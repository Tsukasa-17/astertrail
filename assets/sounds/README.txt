Astertrail SF notification sounds (0.1.280〜)

sf_accept.wav    — soft synth chime for quest/mission accept
sf_complete.wav  — brighter rising arpeggio for complete

These are original short SFX synthesized from math in
dev/gen_sf_sounds.py (numpy). They are not taken from the game,
another addon, or any downloaded sound library.
